Fountain Information Technology Limited was incorporated in England & Wales on 3 March 2008 and has operated as a private limited company ever since.

Our story

Built on independent, practical advice

Fountain Information Technology Limited was founded to give businesses something increasingly rare in IT: consultancy that isn't tied to selling a particular product or platform. Since incorporation in 2008, that has remained the starting point for every engagement — understand the business first, then work out what technology actually needs to do.

We work with organisations that need clear thinking about their systems — whether that's a strategic roadmap, an integration between existing platforms, a cloud migration, or ongoing technical support that doesn't disappear once the invoice is paid.

As a registered UK company (number 06521447), we operate with the accountability and continuity that comes with being on the public record at Companies House — status: Active.
