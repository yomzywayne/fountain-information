What we offer

IT consultancy services

Under SIC code 62020 — information technology consultancy activities — we advise on, build and support the systems that keep a business running.

◆

IT Strategy & Advisory

Independent, vendor-neutral advice on where your technology should be headed. We audit your current setup, identify risk and inefficiency, and produce a practical roadmap tied to business priorities — not a product wishlist.

Typical engagements: IT health checks, technology roadmaps, budget and vendor reviews, digital transformation planning.

▣

Systems Integration

Most businesses run several systems that don't talk to each other. We connect CRM, finance, operations and reporting tools so information flows automatically instead of being re-typed.

Typical engagements: API integrations, data migration, legacy system consolidation, workflow automation.

☁

Cloud & Infrastructure

From first migration to ongoing optimisation, we plan and manage cloud infrastructure that is reliable, secure and proportionate to your budget — not over-engineered.

Typical engagements: cloud migration, infrastructure architecture, backup & disaster recovery, cost optimisation reviews.

⚙

Software & Automation

When an off-the-shelf tool doesn't fit, we design and build the smaller, focused software that does — internal tools, integrations and automations that remove repetitive manual work.

Typical engagements: bespoke internal tools, process automation, custom reporting & dashboards.

🛡

Security & Compliance

Pragmatic reviews of access control, data handling and system resilience, so you understand your actual exposure and can prioritise fixes that matter.

Typical engagements: security & access audits, data protection reviews, business continuity planning.

✆

Ongoing Support & Advisory

Technology decisions don't stop at go-live. We offer retained consultancy so there's someone accountable for how your systems perform as the business grows and changes.

Typical engagements: retained IT advisory, periodic system reviews, ad-hoc technical support.

Engagement model

Simple, flexible ways to work together

Project-based

A defined piece of work — a migration, an integration, a build — scoped, quoted and delivered to an agreed timeline.

Retained advisory

Ongoing access to consultancy time each month, so technology decisions have continuity rather than starting from scratch each time.

One-off review

A focused audit or health check that gives you a clear, independent view of where things stand — with no obligation to continue.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe the problem, not the solution — we'll help you work out what's actually needed.
