What we offer

IT consultancy services

We advise on, build and support the systems that keep a business running.

◆

IT Strategy & Advisory

Independent, vendor-neutral advice on where your technology should be headed. We audit your current setup, identify risk and inefficiency, and produce a practical roadmap tied to business priorities — not a product wishlist.

Typical engagements: IT health checks, technology roadmaps, budget and vendor reviews, digital transformation planning.

▦

Microsoft 365 & Power Platform

We design SharePoint intranets and document management that people actually use, and use Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI to replace spreadsheets, paper forms and manual hand-offs — all built on the Microsoft 365 you already pay for.

Typical engagements: SharePoint intranets & document management, migration from file shares, Power Apps business apps, Power Automate workflows, Power BI dashboards, Teams & Microsoft 365 governance.

▣

Integration & Automation

Most businesses run several systems that don't talk to each other. We connect CRM, finance, operations and reporting tools, and where an off-the-shelf tool doesn't fit, build the focused software that removes repetitive manual work.

Typical engagements: API integrations, data migration, legacy system consolidation, bespoke internal tools, process automation, custom reporting.

☁

Cloud & Security

We plan and manage cloud infrastructure that is reliable, secure and proportionate to your budget, and review access, data handling and resilience so you understand your real exposure and fix what matters first.

Typical engagements: cloud migration, infrastructure architecture, backup & disaster recovery, cost optimisation, security & access audits, data protection reviews, business continuity planning.

✦

AI & Machine Learning

Practical advice on where AI can genuinely help your business — and where it can't. We identify realistic use cases, then build and integrate solutions with proper attention to data quality, privacy and cost.

Typical engagements: AI readiness assessments, AI assistants & chatbots, document processing, predictive models, responsible AI guidance.

✆

Ongoing Support

Technology decisions don't stop at go-live. We offer retained consultancy so there's someone accountable for how your systems perform as the business grows and changes.

Typical engagements: retained IT advisory, periodic system reviews, ad-hoc technical support.

Engagement model

Simple, flexible ways to work together

Project-based

A defined piece of work — a migration, an integration, a build — scoped, quoted and delivered to an agreed timeline.

Retained advisory

Ongoing access to consultancy time each month, so technology decisions have continuity rather than starting from scratch each time.

One-off review

A focused audit or health check that gives you a clear, independent view of where things stand — with no obligation to continue.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe the problem, not the solution — we'll help you work out what's actually needed.
