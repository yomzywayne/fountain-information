FAQ

Questions we're often asked

The things most people want to know before their first call with us.

How do you charge for consultancy work?

It depends on the engagement. Defined pieces of work — a migration, an integration, a build — are scoped and quoted up front. Ongoing advisory work is billed as a retained monthly arrangement. Either way, you'll know the cost and the terms before anything starts.

Do you only work with businesses in London?

We're based in London and a lot of our work is with organisations here, but we work with clients across the UK. Most engagements involve a mix of remote and on-site time, agreed up front based on what the project needs.

How quickly can you start?

For a short review or health check, often within a couple of weeks. Larger projects usually start with a scoping conversation so we can plan the right time and the right people — get in touch and we'll give you a realistic timeline.

What makes you "independent" as a consultancy?

We don't resell hardware, software licences or cloud capacity, so we have no commission riding on which platform or vendor you choose. Our recommendations are based on what fits your business, not what pays us the most.

Do you support systems after a project is finished?

Yes — many clients move from a project engagement into retained advisory or ad-hoc support once the initial work is delivered, so there's continuity as the business and its systems change.

Have a project or a problem to work through?

Tell us what you're dealing with — we'll tell you honestly whether, and how, we can help.
